# Team 45 - Working Agreement & Charter

## 1. Daily Cadence & Communication
* **Syncs:** 1 hour meeting daily.
* **Communication Channel:** WhatsApp and Google Meet

## 2. Board & Commit Rules
* **Anti-Black-Box Rule:** No task card exceeds 4 hours.
* **Commit Format:** Every commit must follow `<type>: <what changed> - <why it matters>`.
* **PR Process:** No direct pushes to `main` without at least 1 team member review.

## 3. Escalation Path for Blockers
* **Trigger:** Trigger: If a disagreement stalls progress for more than 4 hours, or if a team member shows zero board/commit activity for 24+ hours, formal escalation is triggered.
* **Step 1:** Internal Review: The Project Lead convenes a mandatory 15-minute sync with the involved members to review task definitions and Definition of Done.
* **Step 2:** Course Correction: The Project Lead reassigns the blocked task or updates the board card to keep the sprint timeline intact.
* **Step 3:** Governance Logging: The disagreement resolution and any task reassignments are documented in the sprint log for transparency. If unblocked within 2 hours, document the issue as "Known Broken" in `GO_LIVE.md` and pivot to next task.
