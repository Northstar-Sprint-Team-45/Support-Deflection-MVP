# Team 45 - Working Agreement & Charter

## 1. Daily Cadence & Communication
* **Syncs:** Brief 15-minute daily standup to start each day.
* **Communication Channel:** WhatsApp.

## 2. Board & Commit Rules
* **Anti-Black-Box Rule:** No task card exceeds 4 hours.
* **Commit Format:** Every commit must follow `<type>: <what changed> - <why it matters>`.
* **PR Process:** No direct pushes to `main` without at least 1 team member review.

## 3. Escalation Path for Blockers
* **Step 1:** Flag blocker in team chat immediately.
* **Step 2:** If unresolved after 1 hour, assign another team member to pair-program.
* **Step 3:** If unblocked within 2 hours, document the issue as "Known Broken" in `GO_LIVE.md` and pivot to next task.
